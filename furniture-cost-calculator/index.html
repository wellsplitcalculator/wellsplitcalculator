About the Furniture Cost Calculator

Whether you're moving in together and need to split furniture costs fairly, or separating and need to divide shared items, this calculator helps you find a fair solution.

How to use this tool

  • Buy-In Mode: Split costs when moving in together. Track who owns what and calculate buy-in amounts.
  • Buy-Out Mode: Divide furniture during separation. See who keeps what and who owes whom.
  • Add furniture items with costs, ages, categories, and ownership assignment.
  • Choose split method (equal, by income, or custom) and valuation method (original, depreciated, or market value).
  • Get a clear breakdown of each person's share and any buyout amounts.

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between Buy-In and Buy-Out modes?

Buy-In is for when you're moving in together — it helps split new furniture costs and track ownership. Buy-Out is for separation — it helps divide existing furniture and calculate who owes whom.

How should we split furniture costs when moving in together?

Common methods include equal split (50/50), proportional by income (fairer when incomes differ), or custom percentage. The calculator supports all three.

What's a fair depreciation rate for furniture?

10-20% per year is typical. Sofas and mattresses depreciate faster (15-20%), while solid wood furniture holds value better (10-15%).

How is the buyout amount calculated?

The calculator finds the difference in value between what each person is taking. The person taking more pays half the difference to the other person to equalize the division.

What if one person already owns furniture?

Mark those items as owned by that person. The calculator will track their ownership and adjust the split accordingly.

Can we split individual items?

Yes! Mark items as "Shared" and the calculator splits their value according to your chosen split method.

What about items that were gifts?

Gifts are typically the recipient's property. Assign them to that person and the calculator will handle it correctly.

How does income affect the split?

With income-based splitting, higher earners contribute more to shared purchases, which is reflected in the final ownership percentages.

Is this calculator legally binding?

No, this is a planning and negotiation tool. Use it to find a fair solution, but consult a professional for legal agreements.

Is this calculator free to use?

Yes, completely free. We believe fair furniture cost sharing should be accessible to everyone.
