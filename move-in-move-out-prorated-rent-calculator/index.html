About This Prorated Rent Calculator

This calculator handles both move-in and move-out proration scenarios, making it versatile for tenants, landlords, and property managers.

How to use it

  • Move-In: Set your move-in date. Optionally add a move-out date for partial-month stays.
  • Move-Out: Set your move-out date. Optionally add a move-in date for partial-month stays.
  • Choose between actual days or fixed 30-day proration method.
  • Choose whether the move-out day is included in billing.
  • Calculate and copy the results.

Always verify the result against your lease agreement, as proration rules can vary.

Prorated Rent FAQs

Common questions about prorated rent calculations.

What is a prorated rent calculator?

It calculates rent for only the days a tenant occupies a rental during a partial month.

What's the difference between move-in and move-out proration?

Move-in proration starts from the move-in date. Move-out proration ends at the move-out date, typically starting from the 1st.

What daily-rate method should I use?

Use actual days in the month unless your lease requires a fixed 30-day method.

Is the move-in day included?

Yes, the move-in date is always included as a billable day.

Is the move-out day included?

You can choose whether the move-out day is included in billing.

Does this work for February?

Yes. The actual-days method automatically uses 28 or 29 days.

Can I use any currency?

Yes. Select the currency symbol you want displayed.

Is the result legally binding?

No. It is an estimate and should be checked against the lease.
